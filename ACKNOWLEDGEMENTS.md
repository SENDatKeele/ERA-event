# Sharing and continuing your work

If you share work from this event or continue using the data afterwards, please acknowledge the SEND team at Keele University and cite both the data repository and the associated paper. This includes presentations, reports, online posts, code repositories and further research.

## What to include

- Credit Keele University's Smart Energy Network Demonstrator team as the data source.
- Link to the [ERA hackathon repository](https://github.com/SENDatKeele/ERA-event) and identify the annual files and repository commit or download date you used.
- Include the paper citation below. It is currently under review, so describe it as a submitted manuscript.
- Explain any changes you made to the data and distinguish your own analysis from the work you are citing.

You can adapt this wording for your slide, report or repository:

> This work uses campus energy data provided by the Smart Energy Network Demonstrator team at Keele University through the ERA hackathon repository: https://github.com/SENDatKeele/ERA-event. The associated research is described in Akintunde et al., *Green Hydrogen Production Potential Evaluation using the Smart Energy Network Demonstrator Facility at Keele University*, submitted manuscript under review, with accompanying Supplemental Information. The analysis presented here is our own.

## Paper citation

Akintunde, H., Abraham, G., Hirschi, R., Griffiths, A., Bonham, W. D., Creissen, C. E., Dean, M., Matthews, P. D., Ormerod, R. M., Wye, S., and SEND team. *Green Hydrogen Production Potential Evaluation using the Smart Energy Network Demonstrator Facility at Keele University*. Manuscript submitted for publication, under review. Accompanied by *Supplemental Information*.

If a published version is available when you share your work, use its final citation and DOI. If you also use the research code, acknowledge the [GreenHydrogenKeele repository](https://github.com/SENDatKeele/GreenHydrogenKeele).
